Nonprofit · Community Health

Harvest Table Food Access Network

$480,000 secured

The challenge

Harvest Table ran a proven food-access program but had been rejected from two prior state grants. Their internal team was strong on program delivery but had never translated outcomes data into a competitive funding narrative, and deadlines kept arriving faster than proposals could be written.

The solution

Writemygrants ran a full grant research scan to identify a better-matched state funding stream, then rebuilt the proposal narrative around three years of program outcomes data the organization had been collecting but never featured. A revised budget narrative aligned spending directly to funder priorities.

The outcome

The application was funded on its first submission under the new strategy — a two-year grant that let Harvest Table expand from one distribution site to four, and established a funder relationship now supporting a renewal application.

Small Business · Manufacturing Startup

Kestrel Metalworks

$150,000 secured

The challenge

Kestrel Metalworks, an early-stage manufacturing startup, needed non-dilutive capital to purchase equipment but didn't know small business innovation grants existed outside of traditional bank loans or investor funding, and had no experience navigating a federal application.

The solution

A funding strategy engagement identified a state small business innovation grant matched to their equipment and job-creation plans. Writemygrants developed the full proposal package — narrative, budget, and job-impact projections — and prepared the founder for a funder interview.

The outcome

Kestrel was awarded funding that covered over 60% of its planned equipment purchase, accelerating production capacity by nearly a year and creating four new local manufacturing jobs within six months of the award.

Educational Institution · Workforce Program

Ridgeline Community College

$610,000 secured

The challenge

Ridgeline wanted to launch a workforce training program in partnership with local employers but needed multi-year funding to cover curriculum development, equipment, and student stipends — a proposal complex enough that a single grants administrator couldn't take it on alongside existing renewal deadlines.

The solution

Writemygrants managed full proposal development: coordinating letters of support from employer partners, building a multi-year budget across three funding categories, and writing a narrative that framed the program around regional labor-market data.

The outcome

The college secured a three-year workforce development grant, enabling the program to launch a year ahead of schedule with its first employer-partner cohort already enrolled.
